# Kamyar Zeinalipour

I build language models for Europe's languages and for the ones with almost no data, and the data and benchmarks that make them useful. Postdoctoral researcher at the University of Siena (DIISM) and AI engineer at YUKAI! and Villanova.AI.

**Open European LLMs.** At Villanova.AI we train open models in Italy for English, Italian, German, Spanish and French: [Villanova2](https://huggingface.co/collections/VillanovaAI/villanova2-6adf59f613d86d7ccbd654fa). I built [Villanova-SFT-2603](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VillanovaAI/villanova-sft-2603), 1.7 million instruction conversations under Apache 2.0, and co-wrote [Multi-SFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22086).

**Benchmarks for languages with little data.** [ArabicSense](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/ArabicSense), [FarSense](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/FarSense), [Shawarma Chats](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Shawarma-Chats), [PersianMCQ-Instruct](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Persian_MCQ), [Sindhi reasoning](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Sindhi-Reasoning), [TalkFa](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/TalkFa).

**Education.** Crossword generators in [Italian](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Italian-Text-to-Crosswords), [Arabic](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Arabic-Text-to-Crosswords) and [Turkish](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/CW_Clue_Gen_tr), [MultiHint-9K](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/MultiHint-9K), [essay feedback](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Essay-Syntax-Feedback), [Turkish quizzes](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Turkish_Quiz_Generator), [clue difficulty](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/Crossword-Surprisal).

**Science and industry.** [Protein design with LLMs](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/protein-design-LLMs), [PharMistral](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/PharMistral), [PharmaQA.IT](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/PharmaQA.IT), [From Graph to Text and Back](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/round-trip-kg) (ACL 2026), [ts2g](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/ts2g), and emission forecasting for gas turbines at Baker Hughes.

**Teaching.** [Large Language Models for Developers](https://github.com/KamyarZeinalipour/zucchetti-llm-course), a 20-hour course with slides and labs.
